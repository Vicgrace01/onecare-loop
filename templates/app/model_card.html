2. Intended use

These components are operational aids for a care-navigation team. They help a human prioritise which referrals to chase first, and which patient replies mean what. They do not make clinical decisions, they do not recommend treatment, and they do not diagnose.

Not intended for: clinical decision support, triage, diagnosis, ambulance dispatch, or replacing the judgment of a licensed clinician. The product can be operated — and clinically closed — without either component running.

3. Drop-off risk score

Features
  • BP severity (systolic, diastolic)
  • Urgency tier
  • Insurance type (NHIA / self-pay)
  • Patient age band
  • Referring facility type (pharmacy / PMV)
  • Day of week of referral
Deliberately excluded
  • Patient name, phone, address
  • Gender, ethnicity, religion
  • Any free-text field
Training data

Trained on 2,000 synthetically generated patient journeys. Not trained on real patient data. The generator is seeded and reproducible.

Evaluation

AUC 0.79 on held-out synthetic set. This number is not evidence of real-world performance. Field validation is scheduled for the pilot.

Output

A single score between 0 and 1. Surfaced to the care navigator as low / medium / high. Never shown to the patient, never used to gate any state transition.

4. Reply classifier

Classifies patient replies into four buckets: yes, no, reschedule, stop. Two-path implementation:

  • Primary: Groq API with a fixed prompt. The model only classifies; it does not compose replies.
  • Fallback: deterministic keyword matching on the multilingual templates. Always available, even offline.

If the two paths disagree, the human-readable reply is shown to the care navigator and no automated action is taken. Disagreements are logged for review.

5. Ethical considerations

  • Neither model can move a patient along the state machine. Only human-authored transitions do.
  • Neither model is exposed to patients. The patient receipt shows only events and their hashes.
  • If the drop-off model is removed, the product still works — the navigator just loses a prioritisation hint.
  • If the reply classifier is removed, replies are surfaced to the navigator as free text.
  • No model output is used as the sole basis for any state transition, billing event, or clinical outcome.
