Measured metrics

Primary
  • Loop closure rate (baseline vs OneCare)
  • Median time-to-care (screening → disposition)
  • Time-to-accept at hospital
  • Counter-referral completion rate
Secondary
  • Patient SMS reply rate per language
  • Pharmacy staff time-to-screen (target < 90s)
  • Hospital accept latency (target < 4h for emergency)
  • Chain verification count per day

Pre-pilot blockers (must close before Week 1)

  • In-country hosting. Compute currently runs on Render US/EU. Must move to a Lagos or Nigerian-region host (NDPA §41–43).
  • External DPO review of DPIA. Scheduled. Cannot launch without sign-off.
  • Native-speaker review of all non-English templates. All Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, Yorùbá templates must be reviewed by a native speaker before going live to patients.
  • Twilio live credentials. Sandbox is fine for demo; a real shortcode is required for the pilot.
  • Hygeia policy version 1. The benefit structure must be agreed and frozen; changing it mid-pilot invalidates the pilot's comparison baseline.

What the pilot is not

  • Not a clinical trial. No control arm. Not powered for outcomes.
  • Not a substitute for a licensed clinician at any step.
  • Not a production deployment. Bugs, downtime, and manual intervention are expected.
  • Not multi-disease. Hypertension only. That is the whole point.
